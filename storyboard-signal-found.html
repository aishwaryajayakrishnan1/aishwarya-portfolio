THE WAY HOME / FICTIONAL ADOPTION FILM

Kindness can change an address.

I shaped this as an observational public-awareness film rather than a polished pet commercial. The camera notices small, practical acts—stopping, asking an adult, offering water and making a safe place. The emotion grows from behaviour, while the final line turns one family’s choice into a gentle invitation to adopt responsibly.

03 / FIRST YES

Visual direction: Tight detail of paw meeting palm beneath the mother’s umbrella. Hold for one full beat; this is the emotional turn.

04 / TOGETHER

Visual direction: A natural three-shot. The child looks to her mother; the adult checks the collar and the puppy’s condition. Adoption is shown as a shared, responsible decision.

DAUGHTER

05 / CARE

Visual direction: Warm handheld interior. No instant makeover—just a towel, clean water and patient hands. Show care as a sequence of ordinary actions.
