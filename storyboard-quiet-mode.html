QUIET MODE / FICTIONAL DIGITAL-WELLBEING FILM

Put the evening back in your hands.

The product is present for only a few seconds. I designed the film around the change it enables: blue notification light gives way to warm, tactile life. The camera begins static and distant, then becomes closer and more human as attention returns to the room.
